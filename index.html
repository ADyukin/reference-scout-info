Find the right reference in your own collection.

Reference Scout is a read-only AI/MCP assistant for searching design inspiration in the Pinterest boards and Pins you have saved. It helps surface relevant examples for product and interface design research.

01 — Connect

Your Pinterest account stays yours

The integration is intended for the account owner to search their own boards and saved Pins. It does not request access to other people's private collections.

02 — Search

Read-only by design

Reference Scout reads board and Pin details through Pinterest’s official API. It does not create, edit, or delete content on Pinterest.

Reference Scout is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with Pinterest, Inc. Pinterest is a trademark of its respective owner. This informational website does not itself connect to Pinterest or collect Pinterest account data.
